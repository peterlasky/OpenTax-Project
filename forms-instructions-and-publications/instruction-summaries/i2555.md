# I2555

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/instructions/i2555.pdf`

Title: `Instructions for Form 2555 - Foreign Earned Income`

Role in the tax program:

- governs foreign earned income exclusion
- governs housing exclusion or deduction
- affects downstream calculations that depend on excluded-income addbacks or filing of `Form 2555`

High-value implementation takeaways:

- `Form 2555` is not just an informational attachment; it changes downstream tax computation and modified-income style calculations
- the instructions explicitly say the form is used to figure:
  - foreign earned income exclusion
  - housing exclusion or deduction
- the instructions explicitly direct taxpayers to the `Form 1040` instructions `Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet` when excluded income changes line `16` tax
- the instructions also direct taxpayers using `Form 6251` to use the `Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet` in the `Form 6251` instructions
- the filing of `Form 2555` therefore matters both for routed line amounts and for downstream tax-computation worksheet choice

Current model implications:

- `f2555.foreign_earned_income_exclusion` is currently modeled and now flows into `f1040s1a.2b`
- the current model also exposes `f2555.housing_deduction`
- the exact Schedule `1-A` line `2c` mapping still needs tighter source confirmation because the instructions speak in terms of line-level `Form 2555` outputs, while the current model only exposes a simplified housing output
- `Form 1040 - Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet (Line 16)` is already modeled
- the `Form 6251` foreign-earned-income worksheet path is not yet modeled as its own worksheet object

Audit cue:
- whenever `Form 2555` is present, check all three paths:
  - the direct routed amount from `Form 2555`
  - the Schedule `1-A` addback path for lines `45` and `50`
  - the worksheet-selection rule for the `Form 1040` and `Form 6251` foreign earned income tax worksheets
