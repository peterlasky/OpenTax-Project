# P946

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p946.pdf`

Title: `Publication 946 - How To Depreciate Property`

Role in the tax program:

- principal depreciation publication
- governs section 179, bonus depreciation, MACRS, listed property, placed-in-service timing, and depreciation corrections

Core uses:

- recover cost/basis of business or income-producing property over time
- determine section 179 election vs depreciation
- determine special depreciation allowance
- apply MACRS lives/methods/conventions

2025 headline values:

- section 179 limit: `2,500,000`
- section 179 phaseout threshold: `4,000,000`
- section 179 SUV cap: `31,300`
- some pre-`2025-01-20` qualified property gets `40%` bonus (`60%` for some long-production property / aircraft)
- qualified property acquired and placed in service after `2025-01-19` generally regains `100%` bonus depreciation, with election options
- qualified production property placed in service after `2025-07-04` may qualify for `100%` special depreciation allowance

Main modeling distinctions:

- depreciable vs nondepreciable property
- repair vs improvement
- section 179 vs bonus vs regular MACRS
- listed property with personal-use percentages
- placed-in-service date and acquisition date both matter

Important cross-links:

- `Pub. 527`
- `Pub. 587`
- `Pub. 551`
- `Form 4562`
- `Form 3115`

Critical engine implications:

- depreciation depends on:
  - basis
  - business-use percentage
  - asset class/life
  - convention
  - placed-in-service date
  - prior elections
- correction of prior depreciation can require accounting-method logic rather than simple current-year override

Model-building takeaways:

- asset records must capture:
  - description/category
  - acquisition date
  - placed-in-service date
  - cost/basis
  - land split if real property
  - business-use percentage
  - section 179 election
  - bonus depreciation eligibility/election
  - MACRS class life/convention
- depreciation should be asset-level, not one annual aggregate box
