# P925

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p925.pdf`

Title: `Publication 925 - Passive Activity and At-Risk Rules`

Role in the tax program:

- one of the main loss-limitation publications
- critical for rental losses, passthrough losses, material participation, at-risk amounts, and sequencing of deduction limits

Core principle:

- at-risk rules apply before passive-activity rules

What it limits:

- deductible losses from trade, business, rental, and other income-producing activities

Main forms:

- `Form 6198`
- `Form 8582`
- `Form 8582-CR`
- `Form 8960`
- `Form 461`

Important sequencing:

- basis limits first
- then at-risk limits
- then passive loss limits
- then possible excess business loss limitation

Current reminder items:

- excess business loss limitation can still apply after basis / at-risk / passive limits
- regrouping and real-property-trade-or-business definitions updated under regulations
- NIIT can trigger regrouping considerations

Important substantive distinctions:

- passive vs nonpassive activity
- material participation vs no material participation
- rental activity special rules
- special `25,000` allowance for some rental real estate
- at-risk financed amounts vs protected/nonrecourse amounts not at risk

High-value modeling implications:

- passthrough or rental loss amount on an info return is not automatically deductible
- need facts outside the face of the K-1 or Schedule E, especially:
  - material participation
  - grouping elections
  - real-estate-professional status
  - amount at risk
  - suspended loss carryovers

Model-building takeaways:

- preserve activity-level records rather than only annual totals
- capture:
  - entity/activity identity
  - passive/nonpassive status
  - participation facts
  - at-risk basis / debt characterization
  - suspended losses and credits
  - disposition events that may free suspended losses
- this publication is essential whenever rental or K-1 losses appear
