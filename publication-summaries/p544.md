# P544

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p544.pdf`

Title: `Publication 544 - Sales and Other Dispositions of Assets`

Role in the tax program:

- general disposition framework for non-home, non-retirement asset sales and other dispositions
- key guide for gain/loss computation, character, business-property treatment, and reporting

Core covered dispositions:

- sale
- exchange
- condemnation / involuntary conversion
- repossession
- abandonment
- gift / certain transfers
- partial dispositions of some property

Main questions it answers:

- how to figure gain or loss
- whether item is capital or ordinary
- whether loss is deductible
- how to report on return

Major cross-links:

- `Pub. 523` for sale of main home
- `Pub. 537` for installment sales
- `Pub. 547` for casualty/disaster/theft
- `Pub. 550` for stocks/bonds/options and similar investments
- `Pub. 551` for basis
- `Pub. 559` for transfers at death
- `Pub. 4681` for canceled debts / foreclosures / abandonments

Main forms:

- `Schedule D`
- `Form 4797`
- `Form 8824`
- `Form 8949`
- `Form 1099-A`
- `Form 1099-C`

2025 items called out:

- certain qualified production property can now be section `1245` property if designated/elected and placed in service after `2025-07-04`
- basis adjustments for some section `1245` property require attention
- `Form 1099-DA` now exists for digital asset broker proceeds

Important modeling distinctions:

- capital asset vs section `1231` / `1245` / `1250` business property
- taxable sale vs nontaxable exchange
- ordinary income recapture vs capital gain
- deductible loss vs nondeductible personal loss
- foreign-person real-estate disposition withholding issues

Disposition engine implications:

- gain/loss needs:
  - amount realized
  - adjusted basis
  - disposition costs
  - liability assumptions / debt relief
- character needs:
  - asset type
  - holding period
  - business / investment / personal use
  - depreciation claimed

Model-building takeaways:

- do not use a one-size-fits-all “asset sale” rule
- preserve structured facts for:
  - type of disposition
  - asset category
  - holding period
  - recapture exposure
  - related debt cancellation
  - foreign-person / FIRPTA context where relevant
