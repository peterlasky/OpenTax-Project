# P590B

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p590b.pdf`

Title: `Publication 590-B - Distributions from Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs)`

Role in the tax program:

- main distribution-side IRA publication
- governs taxable withdrawals, Roth ordering, inherited IRA rules, RMDs, early-distribution penalties, and disaster-related IRA relief

Core sections:

- traditional IRA withdrawals and RMDs
- Roth IRA withdrawals
- inherited IRA rules
- early distributions and penalties
- disaster-related relief

Important current reminders:

- new RMD rules for certain designated beneficiaries of post-2019 deaths generally require full distribution by end of `10th` year, except for eligible designated beneficiaries
- custodians must generally report or offer to calculate RMD by `January 31`
- IRA earnings are tax-deferred, not tax-exempt interest
- IRA distributions are generally excluded from NIIT itself, though they can raise MAGI for NIIT threshold purposes

Critical modeling distinctions:

- traditional IRA vs Roth IRA
- owner distribution vs beneficiary distribution
- regular distribution vs rollover / recontribution
- early distribution with exception vs subject to additional tax
- corrective distribution vs normal withdrawal

Key interactions:

- contribution basis / excess contributions come from `Pub. 590-A`
- `1099-R`, `W-4P`, and `W-4R` can be relevant operationally
- SEP/SIMPLE rules are not primarily here; see `Pub. 560`

Inherited IRA implications:

- beneficiary status matters materially
- eligible designated beneficiary vs ordinary designated beneficiary vs estate/nonperson beneficiary changes timing rules

Roth implications:

- distribution taxability depends on ordering and qualification rules
- do not assume all Roth withdrawals are automatically tax-free

Model-building takeaways:

- preserve:
  - IRA type
  - basis / nondeductible contribution history where relevant
  - age and exception facts
  - owner death date
  - beneficiary category
  - prior-year RMD status
  - disaster-repayment facts
- separate IRA-distribution logic from pension/annuity logic in `Pub. 575`
