# P530

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p530.pdf`

Title: `Publication 530 - Tax Information for Homeowners`

Role in the tax program:

- main homeowner reference for deductions, credits, closing costs, mortgage interest, real estate taxes, basis adjustments, and records

Main questions it answers:

- what homeowner costs are deductible now
- what costs increase basis instead
- what records should be retained
- what home-related credits may be available

Key 2025 items called out:

- SALT limit increased to `40,000` (`20,000` MFS), with phase-down above `500,000` MAGI (`250,000` MFS), not below `10,000` (`5,000` MFS)
- residential clean energy and energy efficient home improvement credits generally end after `2025`
- residential clean energy credit rate for qualifying property in `2022` through `2025`: `30%`
- mortgage insurance premium deduction expired
- home equity loan interest is not deductible unless proceeds were used to buy, build, or substantially improve the home
- qualified principal residence debt discharge exclusion extended through end of `2025`

Homeowner Assistance Fund rules:

- HAF payments are not income
- taxpayer cannot deduct or credit expenditures paid with HAF funds
- optional method exists for certain itemizers to determine mortgage-interest and real-estate-tax deductions where both taxpayer funds and HAF funds were used

Important modeling distinctions:

- deductible now:
  - some mortgage interest
  - some state/local real estate taxes
  - certain energy credits
- not deductible now but basis-affecting:
  - many settlement / closing costs
  - capital improvements
- not deductible:
  - personal expenses
  - disallowed home equity interest
  - expired mortgage insurance premium deduction

Basis rules matter:

- basis must be tracked over time
- improvements and certain debt-discharge exclusions affect basis
- records should support original cost and adjusted basis
- basis is critical later for `Pub. 523` home sale computation

Important forms/cross-links:

- `Schedule A`
- `Form 5695`
- `Form 982`
- `Pub. 523`
- `Pub. 936`
- `Pub. 551`
- `Pub. 4681`

Mortgage debt forgiveness rule:

- excluded qualified principal residence debt forgiveness reduces home basis, but not below zero

Model-building takeaways:

- separate homeowner data into:
  - current-year deductible expenses
  - current-year credits
  - nondeductible personal costs
  - basis adjustments
- do not merge `Pub. 530` homeowner rules with rental-property rules from `Pub. 527`
- preserve enough detail to later support home-sale exclusion and basis computation
