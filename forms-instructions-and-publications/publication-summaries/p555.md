# P555

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p555.pdf`

Title: `Publication 555 - Community Property`

Role in the tax program:

- controls income/property allocation when taxpayers are subject to community property law
- especially important for MFS returns, certain partner situations, and basis after spouse death

Who it applies to:

- married taxpayers domiciled in community property states
- also certain registered domestic partners in California, Nevada, and Washington

Community property states listed:

- Arizona
- California
- Idaho
- Louisiana
- Nevada
- New Mexico
- Texas
- Washington
- Wisconsin

Core rule:

- if filing separately, taxpayer generally reports:
  - half of all community income
  - all separate income

Important form:

- `Form 8958` for allocation of tax amounts between certain individuals in community property states

Key distinctions:

- domicile controls whether community-property law applies
- community property vs separate property
- community income vs separate income
- end-of-community rules after divorce, separation, or death

Important reminders in publication:

- same-sex marriages are treated the same as opposite-sex marriages for federal tax purposes
- registered domestic partnerships are not marriages for federal tax purposes, but community-property allocation can still apply in certain states
- alimony under post-2018 instruments is generally not deductible/includible

Critical modeling implications:

- filing status alone is not enough
- state of domicile and marital facts can change the income split materially
- wages, business income, deductions, withholding, and credits may need allocation rather than simple ownership assignment

Basis implication:

- community-property rules also affect basis in inherited property when spouse dies

Model-building takeaways:

- collect:
  - domicile state
  - move-in / move-out dates if domicile changed during year
  - whether item is community or separate
  - spouse / RDP allocation facts
- do not auto-assign all income to the named recipient on MFS returns in community-property states
- if community-property rules apply, preserve allocation worksheet logic comparable to `Form 8958`
