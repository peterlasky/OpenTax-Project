# P596

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p596.pdf`

Title: `Publication 596 - Earned Income Credit (EIC)`

Role in the tax program:

- primary EIC eligibility and computation guide
- essential for qualification tests, qualifying-child logic, investment-income limit, and edge-case worksheets

Core structure:

- rules for everyone
- extra rules if taxpayer has a qualifying child
- extra rules if taxpayer does not have a qualifying child
- figuring/claiming the credit
- disallowance rules

Key 2025 limits from front matter:

- investment income limit: `11,950`
- AGI / earned income limits:
  - `61,555` (`68,675` MFJ) with 3+ qualifying children
  - `57,310` (`64,430` MFJ) with 2 qualifying children
  - `50,434` (`57,554` MFJ) with 1 qualifying child
  - `19,104` (`26,214` MFJ) with no qualifying child

Core universal rules:

- valid SSN by return due date
- U.S. citizen or resident alien all year
- cannot file `Form 2555`
- must have earned income

Qualifying-child rules:

- relationship test
- age test
- residency test
- joint return test
- tie-breaker / no double-use by multiple taxpayers

No-qualifying-child rules:

- age requirement
- cannot be dependent of another person
- cannot be qualifying child of another taxpayer
- must live in the U.S. more than half the year

Important worksheet paths from the source:

- `Worksheet 1. Investment Income` is required for certain taxpayers filing `Form 1040` or `1040-SR` instead of relying only on Step `2` in the `Form 1040` instructions
- the source specifically lists these triggers for `Worksheet 1`:
  - `Schedule E (Form 1040)`
  - rental of personal property not used in a trade or business
  - `Schedule 1 (Form 1040)`, line `8z`, amounts from `Form 8814`
  - passive-activity income or loss
  - `Form 1040` / `1040-SR`, line `7a`, including an amount from `Form 4797`
- `Worksheet 2. Worksheet for Line 4 of Worksheet 1` is used only when `Form 8814` includes an Alaska Permanent Fund dividend
- both publication worksheets are marked `Keep for Your Records`

Model-building takeaways:

- EIC must be eligibility-driven, not just table-driven
- preserve:
  - SSN validity
  - filing status / separation facts
  - earned income
  - AGI
  - investment income
  - qualifying-child identity and tie-breaker data
  - foreign-earned-income filing flag
- connect EIC logic with dependent/residency engine and info-return income classification
- keep the publication-only `Investment Income` worksheet path distinct from the ordinary `Form 1040` EIC worksheet path
