# P559

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p559.pdf`

Title: `Publication 559 - Survivors, Executors, and Administrators`

Role in the tax program:

- primary reference for post-death filing workflow
- covers decedent’s final `Form 1040`, estate `Form 1041`, income in respect of a decedent, survivor benefits, and estate/gift tax touchpoints

Main responsibilities addressed:

- personal representative duties
- final income tax return for decedent
- estate income tax return
- distributions to beneficiaries
- IRD / deductions in respect of decedent
- estate and gift tax overview

Key forms:

- `Form 1040`
- `Form 1040-SR`
- `Form 1041`
- `Form 706`
- `Form 709`
- `Form 1310`
- `Form 56`
- `Form SS-4`

Important 2025 reminders:

- excess deductions on termination of estate/trust retain separate character categories
- beneficiaries generally must treat estate/trust items consistently with estate return
- consistent basis reporting between estate and property recipient is required in applicable cases
- filing status name is now `qualifying surviving spouse`
- simplified extension exists in some cases to elect portability of DSUE

Critical modeling distinctions:

- decedent’s final return vs estate return vs beneficiary reporting
- income earned before death vs after death
- items reportable by estate vs directly by survivor / beneficiary
- tax-free inheritance vs taxable IRD

IRD implications:

- some amounts are not eliminated at death and remain taxable when later received
- do not treat every post-death receipt as tax-free inheritance

Estate workflow implications:

- decedent return may require special signature/refund procedures
- estate may need EIN and separate filing
- beneficiary distributions can carry income character

Model-building takeaways:

- if taxpayer dies during year, switch to a specialized workflow
- capture:
  - date of death
  - representative/executor details
  - pre-death vs post-death income timing
  - estate existence and EIN
  - beneficiary distributions
  - portability / estate-tax relevance where applicable
- maintain separate engines for:
  - final individual return
  - estate fiduciary return
  - beneficiary carryout amounts
