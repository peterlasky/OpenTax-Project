# P915

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p915.pdf`

Title: `Publication 915 - Social Security and Equivalent Railroad Retirement Benefits`

Role in the tax program:

- primary guide for taxation of Social Security benefits and the SSEB portion of tier 1 railroad retirement benefits
- needed for taxable-benefit computation, lump-sum election handling, and repayment-related relief

Covered benefits:

- Social Security retirement benefits
- Social Security survivor benefits
- Social Security disability benefits
- SSEB portion of tier 1 railroad retirement benefits

Not covered here:

- SSI is not taxable
- non-SSEB railroad benefits belong mainly in `Pub. 575`
- foreign social security benefits follow different rules

Core taxability framework:

- compare base amount for filing status against:
  - one-half of benefits
  - plus all other income, including tax-exempt interest

Important exclusions note:

- in this comparison, do not reduce other income by certain exclusions such as foreign earned income exclusion, education-loan interest, qualified savings-bond interest, adoption benefits, or certain territorial exclusions

Important workflow items:

- `Form SSA-1099`
- `Form SSA-1042S`
- `Form RRB-1099`
- `Form RRB-1042S`
- voluntary withholding can use `Form W-4V`
- estimated tax can matter if withholding is insufficient

Special topics:

- lump-sum election for prior-year benefits paid in current year
- deductions/credits if benefit repayments exceed gross benefits
- children’s benefits follow same general framework but “who is taxed” matters

Important worksheet paths from the source:

- `Worksheet 1. Figuring Your Taxable Benefits`
- `Worksheet 2. Figure Your Additional Taxable Benefits (From a Lump-Sum Payment for a Year After 1993)`
- `Worksheet 3. Figure Your Additional Taxable Benefits (From a Lump-Sum Payment for a Year Before 1994)`
- `Worksheet 4. Figure Your Taxable Benefits Under the Lump-Sum Election Method (Use With Worksheet 2 or 3)`
- the publication marks these worksheets `Keep for Your Records`

Model-building takeaways:

- do not treat SSA benefits as always nontaxable or always 85% taxable
- preserve:
  - gross benefits
  - Medicare withholding
  - prior-year lump-sum amounts
  - filing status / living-apart spouse facts
  - tax-exempt interest
  - other income used in provisional-income style calculation
  - repayment facts
- keep railroad-benefit type distinctions explicit so SSEB is not mixed with taxable pension portions
