# P575

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p575.pdf`

Title: `Publication 575 - Pension and Annuity Income`

Role in the tax program:

- core reference for non-IRA retirement distributions
- drives taxable vs nontaxable pension/annuity amounts, rollovers, early-distribution penalties, survivor treatment, and railroad-retirement non-social-security portions

Core covered topics:

- periodic vs nonperiodic payments
- cost / investment in contract
- partially taxable vs fully taxable payments
- disability pensions
- rollovers
- lump-sum distributions
- early-distribution tax
- excess accumulation tax
- survivors and beneficiaries

Important boundaries:

- IRA distribution rules belong mainly in `Pub. 590-B`
- Social Security and equivalent tier 1 railroad benefits belong mainly in `Pub. 915`
- federal civil service benefits belong mainly in `Pub. 721`

Current reminder items:

- certain domestic-abuse-victim distributions can avoid `10%` early-distribution tax if requirements met
- certain emergency personal expense distributions can avoid `10%` early-distribution tax
- some transfers/rollovers under substantially equal periodic payment method do not count as a modification if requirements met
- retired public safety officers can exclude up to `3,000` for qualifying health / long-term-care insurance premiums

Critical modeling distinctions:

- periodic annuity stream vs nonperiodic withdrawal
- qualified plan vs nonqualified annuity
- taxable portion vs basis recovery
- rollover vs taxable distribution
- early distribution with exception vs without exception

Key workflow inputs:

- `Form 1099-R`
- payer type / plan type
- code on the form
- employee after-tax contributions / investment in contract
- start date and survivor facts

Model-building takeaways:

- do not simply map `1099-R gross = taxable`
- preserve:
  - gross distribution
  - taxable amount if provided
  - basis / after-tax contribution facts
  - rollover amounts
  - exception-to-penalty facts
  - beneficiary/survivor status
- coordinate with `Pub. 590-B` and `Pub. 915` to avoid mixing retirement regimes
