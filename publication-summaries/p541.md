# P541

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p541.pdf`

Title: `Publication 541 - Partnerships`

Role in the tax program:

- partnership and partner reference for pass-through federal income tax treatment
- important for K-1 intake, partner-level basis/limitation reasoning, entity classification, and foreign-partner withholding issues

Core rule:

- partnership generally does not pay income tax itself
- profits, losses, deductions, credits, and other items pass through to partners
- partners report partnership items on their own returns

Important entity-classification rules:

- unincorporated organization with two or more members is generally taxed as partnership unless excluded or election changes status
- simple co-ownership sharing expenses is not necessarily a partnership
- domestic multi-member LLC generally defaults to partnership treatment unless `Form 8832` election changes classification

Key compliance/workflow areas:

- `Form 1065`
- `Schedule K-1`
- `Schedule K-2` / `K-3`
- partnership representative / centralized partnership audit regime

Foreign-partner rules called out:

- withholding may apply on FDAP income
- FIRPTA withholding may apply on acquisition of U.S. real property interest from foreign person
- `1446(a)` withholding can apply to ECTI allocable to foreign partners
- `1446(f)` withholding can apply on foreign partner sale of partnership interest
- FATCA withholding can also apply in some cases

Important modeling distinctions for individual-return engine:

- K-1 intake is not just one net number
- preserve separately:
  - ordinary business income/loss
  - rental items
  - interest/dividends/capital gains
  - credits
  - section-specific disclosures
  - foreign items
- partner-level limits often depend on facts outside the K-1 face amount

Cross-reference implications:

- business expense details may refer to old `Pub. 535`
- basis rules connect to `Pub. 551`
- passive limits connect to `Pub. 925`
- depreciation connects to `Pub. 946`
- installment sales and dispositions connect to `Pub. 537` / `Pub. 544`

Model-building takeaways:

- build K-1 ingestion as structured fields, not a single “partnership income” box
- reserve room for partner-level computations that depend on:
  - basis
  - at-risk limits
  - passive limitations
  - foreign withholding / credits
- flag when foreign-partner or international K-2/K-3 data exists, because it may exceed basic domestic-return handling
