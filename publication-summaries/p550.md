# P550

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p550.pdf`

Title: `Publication 550 - Investment Income and Expenses (Including Capital Gains and Losses)`

Role in the tax program:

- core reference for taxable investment income, investment expenses, capital gains/losses, and many brokerage-account issues
- central for `Schedule B`, `Form 8949`, `Schedule D`, mutual funds, debt instruments, investment interest, and securities basis/holding period rules

Core topic groups:

- interest income
- debt discount
- dividends and distributions
- investment expenses
- sales/trades of investment property
- capital gains and losses
- related special rules for traders / shelters / reportable transactions

Core income types:

- interest
- dividends
- mutual fund distributions
- capital gains
- other investment distributions

Core expense types:

- investment interest expense
- certain costs to manage or collect investment income
- bond premium amortization

High-value 2025 items called out:

- SALT deduction cap increased to `40,000` (`20,000` MFS)
- foreign-source investment income remains reportable unless exempt
- employee stock option taxation belongs partly with `Pub. 525`
- reportable-transaction / micro-captive disclosure rules updated

Important modeling distinctions:

- investment assets vs retirement-account assets
- ordinary dividends vs qualified dividends
- tax-exempt interest vs taxable interest
- OID / market discount / bond premium issues
- wash-sale / basis / holding-period nuances
- trader/business treatment vs ordinary investor treatment

Retirement-plan boundary:

- publication expressly does not govern investments inside IRAs and qualified retirement plans
- use `Pub. 575`, `590-A`, and `590-B` for retirement-plan rules

Main cross-links:

- `Pub. 525`
- `Pub. 541`
- `Pub. 555`
- `Pub. 559`
- `Pub. 925`
- `Pub. 590-B`

Core forms likely implicated:

- `Schedule B`
- `Form 8949`
- `Schedule D`
- `Form 4952`
- numerous broker information returns including digital asset reporting where relevant

Model-building takeaways:

- build investment intake with fine-grained categories, not one “brokerage income” bucket
- preserve:
  - payer / asset type
  - holding period
  - basis method
  - covered vs noncovered security context
  - dividend character
  - tax-exempt vs taxable nature
  - investment-interest-expense facts
- keep retirement assets on a separate rules engine
