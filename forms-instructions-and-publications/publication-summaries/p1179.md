# P1179

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p1179.pdf`

Title: `Publication 1179 - General Rules and Specifications for Substitute Forms 1096, 1098, 1099, 5498, and Certain Other Information Returns`

Role in the tax program:

- implementation-oriented specification for substitute information returns rather than ordinary taxpayer-facing return math
- most relevant when validating printable layouts, recipient copies, and acceptable substitute forms for the `1098`/`1099` family and related statements
- secondary source for the current product unless the app starts generating substitute information returns directly

Front-of-document signals:

- Part 1 – GENERAL INFORMATION
- Section 1.1 – Overview of Revenue Procedure 2025-22 / What's New
- Section 1.2 – Definitions
- Section 1.3 – General Requirements for Acceptable Substitute Forms 1096, 1097-
- BTC, 1098, 1099, 3921, 3922, 5498, W-2G, and 1042-S

Implementation takeaways:

- if the app ever emits recipient or IRS-filing copies for information returns, this publication becomes a layout/spec compliance source
- the document is more about acceptable form reproduction and delivery rules than about tax liability calculations
- use it to separate taxpayer-data modeling from print-format or statement-format requirements
