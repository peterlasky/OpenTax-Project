# P523

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p523.pdf`

Title: `Publication 523 - Selling Your Home`

Role in the tax program:

- primary guide for sale of principal residence
- drives gain exclusion, partial exclusion, basis adjustments, reporting, and special home-sale edge cases

Core exclusion rule:

- up to `250,000` gain exclusion for qualifying taxpayer
- up to `500,000` for qualifying MFJ couple

Main questions this publication answers:

- does the home sale qualify for exclusion
- how much gain or loss exists
- how much of gain is taxable
- how to report on return

High-value build rules:

- principal-residence sale is not a generic capital-asset sale
- must test:
  - ownership
  - use
  - frequency / prior exclusion use
  - special situations for partial exclusion
- publication explicitly names three keep-for-records worksheets:
  - `Worksheet 1. Find Your Exclusion Limit`
  - `Worksheet 2. How To Figure Your Gain or Loss`
  - `Worksheet 3. Determine if You Have Taxable Gain`
- `Worksheet 3` also carries the reporting bridge after taxable-gain determination

2025 items called out:

- some home energy credits require manufacturer identification info beginning in 2025
- certain energy credits expire after 2025
- exclusion for canceled / forgiven mortgage debt on qualified principal residence extended through `2025`

Special interactions:

- mortgage debt cancellation may create ordinary income unless exclusion applies
- depreciation recapture can apply where depreciation was claimed on home-related property
- opportunity-zone rules can interact if capital gain is reinvested

Important cross-references:

- `Pub. 504` for divorce/separation interactions
- `Pub. 505` for withholding/estimated tax after sale
- `Pub. 527` for rental history
- `Pub. 530` for homeowner items
- `Pub. 544` for disposition framework
- `Pub. 547` for casualty basis adjustments
- `Pub. 551` for basis
- `Pub. 587` for business-use home
- `Pub. 936` for mortgage interest
- `Form 4797`, `8949`, `Schedule D`, `982`, `6252`

Critical modeling distinctions:

- residence use vs rental/investment/business use
- exclusion-eligible gain vs non-excludable gain
- depreciation recapture / unrecaptured gain
- canceled debt income vs exclusion
- installment sale vs ordinary one-time sale

Divorce / spouse transfer rule:

- transfer of home to spouse or ex-spouse in divorce settlement is generally no gain / no loss
- but nonresident-alien ex-spouse can break the usual nonrecognition assumption

Model-building takeaways:

- keep home-sale logic separate from generic asset sale logic
- preserve:
  - acquisition/improvement basis data
  - ownership/use periods
  - prior home-sale exclusion usage
  - rental/business use history
  - depreciation claimed
  - debt cancellation facts
- if home was ever rental or business property, cross-link to `Pub. 527`, `Pub. 587`, and `Pub. 544`
