# P936

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p936.pdf`

Title: `Publication 936 - Home Mortgage Interest Deduction`

Role in the tax program:

- primary guide for deductibility limits on home mortgage interest and points
- central for Schedule A itemized-deduction treatment of acquisition debt on main home and second home

Core rules:

- taxpayer must itemize on `Schedule A`
- debt must be secured by a qualified home in which taxpayer has an ownership interest
- both borrower and lender must intend repayment

2025 key limits/reminders:

- mortgage insurance premium deduction expired
- home equity interest is deductible only to extent proceeds were used to buy, build, or substantially improve the home securing the loan
- post-2017 acquisition debt limit generally:
  - `750,000`
  - `375,000` if MFS
- pre-2017 acquisition debt limit generally:
  - `1,000,000`
  - `500,000` if MFS

Debt categories emphasized:

- grandfathered debt
- pre-`2017-12-16` home acquisition debt
- post-`2017-12-15` home acquisition debt

Important modeling distinctions:

- main home vs second home
- secured debt vs unsecured loan
- acquisition/improvement use vs unrelated use of proceeds
- fully deductible interest vs limited interest
- points vs regular interest

Cross-links:

- `Pub. 504`
- `Pub. 523`
- `Pub. 527`
- `Pub. 530`

Model-building takeaways:

- preserve loan-level facts:
  - origination date
  - secured property
  - use of proceeds
  - outstanding principal through year
  - whether loan refinanced older grandfathered debt
  - points paid and by whom
- do not let all `Form 1098` mortgage interest flow straight to deduction without acquisition-debt and use-of-proceeds checks
