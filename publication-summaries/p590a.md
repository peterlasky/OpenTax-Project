# P590A

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p590a.pdf`

Title: `Publication 590-A - Contributions to Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs)`

Role in the tax program:

- main contribution-side IRA publication
- governs traditional IRA contributions, Roth contributions, deduction limits, MAGI phaseouts, roll-ins to IRAs, inherited-IRA contribution context, and saver’s credit reference

Core sections:

- traditional IRAs
- Roth IRAs
- saver’s credit

2025 headline values:

- IRA contribution limit:
  - `7,000`
  - `8,000` if age `50+`

2025 traditional IRA deduction phaseouts for taxpayer covered by workplace plan:

- MFJ / QSS: phaseout above `126,000`, no deduction at `146,000`
- single / HOH: phaseout above `79,000`, no deduction at `89,000`
- MFS: phaseout under `10,000`

2025 traditional IRA deduction phaseout if taxpayer not covered but spouse is:

- phaseout above `236,000`
- no deduction at `246,000`

2025 Roth IRA contribution phaseouts:

- MFJ / QSS:
  - reduced at `236,000`
  - no contribution at `246,000`
- single / HOH / qualifying MFS not living with spouse:
  - reduced at `150,000`
  - no contribution at `165,000`
- MFS living with spouse:
  - reduced above `0`
  - no contribution at `10,000`

Special 2025 item:

- new `Trump account` concept and `Form 4547` noted for eligible children and pilot contribution election

Other important reminders:

- 529-to-Roth rollover path exists subject to restrictions
- RMD age reminder points to age `73`
- qualified disaster relief and certain repayment rules cross over with `Pub. 590-B`
- corrective distribution of excess contribution made by due date avoids `10%` additional tax on that correction

Critical modeling distinctions:

- eligibility to contribute
- amount allowed to contribute
- amount deductible
- traditional vs Roth
- taxpayer covered by plan at work vs spouse covered vs neither

Model-building takeaways:

- preserve:
  - age
  - earned compensation
  - filing status
  - MAGI for IRA purposes
  - workplace-plan coverage for taxpayer and spouse
  - prior excess contributions / corrective distributions
  - 529 rollover facts
- keep contribution logic separate from distribution logic in `Pub. 590-B`

Important worksheet paths from the source:

- when IRA deduction interacts with Social Security taxation, `Appendix B` gives a chained worksheet path:
  - `Worksheet 1. Computation of Modified AGI (For use only by taxpayers who receive social security benefits)`
  - `Worksheet 2` to figure how much traditional IRA contribution can be deducted
  - `Worksheet 3` to figure how much Social Security is taxable after the IRA deduction interaction
- these worksheets are not general-purpose IRA contribution worksheets; they are special-case coordination worksheets for the IRA/Social Security overlap
