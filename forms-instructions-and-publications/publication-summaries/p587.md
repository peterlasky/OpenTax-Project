# P587

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p587.pdf`

Title: `Publication 587 - Business Use of Your Home (Including Use by Daycare Providers)`

Role in the tax program:

- primary guide for home-office deduction and related home-business expense allocation
- also covers inventory-storage exception, daycare exception, and sale of home partly used for business

Core qualification tests:

- exclusive and regular use as principal place of business
- exclusive and regular use to meet/deal with clients/customers
- use of separate unattached structure for business
- special rules for storage of inventory/product samples
- special rules for daycare facilities

Critical rule:

- most home-office deductions require exclusive use
- key exceptions:
  - storage of inventory/product samples
  - daycare facility use

Methods:

- actual-expense method
- simplified method

Important 2025 items:

- SALT cap increased to `40,000` (`20,000` MFS), phased down above `500,000` MAGI (`250,000` MFS), not below `10,000` (`5,000` MFS)
- standard meal/snack rates updated for family daycare and explicitly applicable to territories

Main forms / cross-links:

- `Schedule C`
- `Form 8829`
- `Form 4562`
- `Pub. 523`
- `Pub. 551`
- `Pub. 946`
- `Pub. 527` for rental use

Important modeling distinctions:

- home office vs rental use
- exclusive-use qualified area vs mixed-use area
- actual expenses vs simplified method
- direct expenses vs indirect expenses
- self-employed / partner / farmer contexts differ in reporting path

Sale-of-home interaction:

- business use of home can affect later home-sale and basis/depreciation treatment

Model-building takeaways:

- capture:
  - square footage or area metrics
  - principal-place-of-business facts
  - client-meeting use
  - exclusive-use status
  - daycare/storage exceptions
  - actual vs simplified method election
  - depreciation and prior-year carryover facts
- do not let users claim home-office deduction without an explicit qualification path
