# P974

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p974.pdf`

Title: `Publication 974 - Premium Tax Credit (PTC)`

Role in the tax program:

- primary detailed guide for ACA Marketplace premium tax credit
- essential for `Form 8962`, APTC reconciliation, SLCSP determination, QSEHRA interaction, and special tax-family allocation cases

Core rule:

- PTC helps pay premiums for qualified Marketplace health plans
- if APTC was paid, taxpayer must file `Form 8962` to reconcile APTC with allowed PTC

Current important reminders:

- 2025 coverage-month definition changed so some months can still count even if part of premium is unpaid, if paid amount is enough to avoid coverage termination in specified scenarios
- taxpayers above `400%` of federal poverty line may still qualify for PTC in 2025
- affordability test for family members under employer coverage is based on family coverage cost, not employee-only cost
- reporting entities may not automatically send `1095-B` / `1095-C` unless requested

QSEHRA rule:

- if QSEHRA is affordable for a month, no PTC for that month
- if not affordable, taxpayer may still qualify but monthly PTC is reduced by monthly permitted benefit

Important change-reporting concept:

- taxpayers receiving APTC should promptly report changes to Marketplace:
  - income
  - address
  - other coverage
  - employment
  - birth/adoption
  - marriage/divorce
  - tax-family composition changes

Special situations this publication emphasizes:

- MFS due to domestic abuse or spousal abandonment
- policy covering people outside taxpayer’s tax family
- one policy covering three or more tax families
- marriage during year alternative calculation
- self-employed health insurance deduction interaction
- not-lawfully-present policy allocation cases
- SLCSP determination problems

Main forms:

- `Form 8962`
- `Form 1095-A`
- `Form 7206`

Model-building takeaways:

- PTC is not just a lookup from `1095-A`; it is a reconciliation and allocation engine
- preserve:
  - household income
  - tax-family composition by month
  - Marketplace enrollment months
  - SLCSP amounts
  - APTC paid
  - employer-coverage affordability facts
  - QSEHRA facts
  - self-employed insurance deduction interaction
- monthly granularity matters for correct PTC/APTC results
