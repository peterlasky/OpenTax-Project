# P554

Source file: `forms-instructions-and-publications/publications/p554.pdf`

Title: `Publication 554 - Tax Guide for Seniors`

Role in the tax program:

- overview publication for older taxpayers
- useful for filing thresholds, senior-specific deductions/credits, retirement-income reporting touchpoints, and age-based rule prompts

Main topics:

- whether return must be filed
- taxable vs nontaxable income for seniors
- social security / railroad retirement taxation
- higher filing thresholds and deductions
- credit for elderly or disabled
- home sale and retirement-related topics by reference

2025 items called out:

- enhanced deduction for seniors:
  - up to `6,000` per eligible person
  - up to `12,000` if MFJ and both eligible
- standard deduction amounts:
  - single / MFS: `15,750`
  - MFJ / QSS: `31,500`
  - HOH: `23,625`
- AMT exemption:
  - single: `88,100`
  - MFJ / QSS: `137,000`
  - MFS: `68,500`
- EIC income thresholds listed
- medical mileage rate: `21 cents` per mile

Retirement-related reminders:

- first RMD generally due by `April 1` of year after reaching age `73` for applicable taxpayers
- age restriction for traditional IRA contributions has been removed
- qualified disaster retirement relief can involve `Form 8915-F`

Important modeling implications:

- age is not just demographic data; it changes:
  - filing threshold
  - standard deduction
  - senior enhanced deduction
  - some credits and retirement rules
- senior returns often mix:
  - SSA / RRB benefits
  - pensions / annuities
  - IRA distributions
  - medical deductions
  - home sale / survivor issues

Forms/cross-links:

- `Form 1040-SR`
- `Pub. 915`
- `Pub. 575`
- `Pub. 590-A`
- `Pub. 590-B`

Model-building takeaways:

- preserve exact DOB and spouse DOB because many senior rules are age-triggered
- prompt for retirement-income and social-security sources early for older taxpayers
- use this publication as an index/overview, but defer specialized calculations to narrower publications when available
